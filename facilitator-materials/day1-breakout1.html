Q2 · tagging, worked

What it sounds like

You name which of the six the item is about, out loud, so the notetaker types your decision instead of guessing one. A tag names the subject, not the problem.The test: “this one is about ___.” The blank is the tag. If it will not fill, say “other”, or say nothing.
“People think the library only wants the final dataset. We’d rather hear from them at the proposal stage.”→ “That’s an ownership and referral one.”
“Everyone assumes IT storage is infinite and free.”→ “Infrastructure and tools, that one.”
“People think ethics is a form you do at the start, not something running through the project.”→ “Policy and compliance.”
“People think I’m the one who fixes the printer.”→ say nothing. It does not fit one of the six, and forcing it is worse than leaving it.
Two or three of the seven, not all of them. Woven in as a short aside while the group is still on it, then straight to the next person. You are summarising, not announcing.

The six tags

A tag names the subject, not the problem. So a gap and an overlap take the same tag: “nobody owns the handoff” and “we both do it and never compare notes” are both ownership and referral. Same question, opposite answer.
Ownership and referralwhose job is it, and where does someone go first
Training and awarenessthey don’t know it exists, or don’t know how
Capacity and staffingnobody has the time or the position
Infrastructure and toolsstorage, platforms, software, documentation and deposit
Policy and complianceTri-Agency, institutional policy, ethics and sensitive data
Regional communityshared resources, contacts, community of practice
Othersay it, the notetaker writes it in
Unsure? Say “other”, or say nothing. A blank is honest; a wrong tag is worse, because nobody downstream can tell.

Someone is taking up too much room

“That’s useful, and I want to hear how that lands for a smaller shop.”
Thank them specifically, then redirect. The cleanest move there is.

Someone hasn’t spoken

“Number 5, you’ve been thinking about something.”
Numbers are less exposing than names. And after any card write, go round in order: nobody has to volunteer, and the quiet people have already written their answer.

Keep a round short

“Just read them out, no explaining yet. The next question is where we dig in.”
A constraint with a promise attached reads as a plan, not a brush-off.
