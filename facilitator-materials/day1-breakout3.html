The six tags

A tag names the subject, not the problem. So a gap and an overlap take the same tag: “nobody owns the handoff” and “we both do it and never compare notes” are both ownership and referral. Same question, opposite answer.
Ownership and referralwhose job is it, and where does someone go first
Training and awarenessthey don’t know it exists, or don’t know how
Capacity and staffingnobody has the time or the position
Infrastructure and toolsstorage, platforms, software, documentation and deposit
Policy and complianceTri-Agency, institutional policy, ethics and sensitive data
Regional communityshared resources, contacts, community of practice
Othersay it, the notetaker writes it in
Unsure? Say “other”, or say nothing. A blank is honest; a wrong tag is worse, because nobody downstream can tell.

Someone hasn’t spoken

“Number 5, you’ve been thinking about something.”
Numbers are less exposing than names. And after any card write, go round in order: nobody has to volunteer, and the quiet people have already written their answer.
